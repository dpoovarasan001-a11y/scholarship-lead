BEFORE YOU APPLY, VERIFY WHAT MATTERS.

A scholarship platform should not rely only on uploaded files. Where verification is supported, documents should be checked against appropriate official or authorized verification sources before they are treated as verified.

⚠
INSTITUTIONAL VERIFICATION RULE #SEC-77

Do not treat a QR code alone as proof that a document is authentic. A QR code should only be considered part of verification when it can be validated through the appropriate issuing authority or authorized verification mechanism.
